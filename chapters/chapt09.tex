\section{I Explore}
Earth appears to be round when viewed form space, but its shape is closer to that of an oblate sphariod. It flattens at the poles and buldges out at the Equator. It has no corners, no beginning and no end. So, it is very difficult to locate places on Earth. Therefore, we use some imaginary fixed points and lines on the earth's surface to help us locate different places on it.
\vspace{0.1in}
\par
The North Pole, the South Pole and the Equator are such fixed points and lines.
